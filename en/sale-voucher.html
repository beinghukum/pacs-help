Sale Voucher

Makes the sale voucher of every permit that is still waiting for one on PACS - one permit per Save, oldest first. You type nothing in this sheet: it is the app's log of the vouchers it made.

1. What this module does

It works on the PACS page Merchandise → Sale Voucher. It reads PACS's list of permits waiting for a voucher (for your Godown and Payment), and for each permit - oldest first - it ticks it, saves the voucher and checks that the permit has left the waiting list. Every voucher gets a row in the sheet.

PACS Auto only prepares (saves) the sale voucher. Posting the voucher is always done by you on PACS.

2. Before you start

  • Logged in to PACS - green Logged in (see Getting started).
  • The permits are made (for example with Khad Sale), or the returns with Sale Return.

Sale Voucher - ribbon
Check pending voucherRibbon · Sale Voucher
↑ picture

Shows the permits still waiting for a sale voucher on PACS for the chosen Activity, Godown and Payment - Permit No, date and amount, oldest first. Read only - nothing is saved.

When to use

  • Before Make voucher - to see how many permits and how much will be made.
  • After a CHECK row - to let the app look again.

What happens

  • The window lists the permits with their total.
  • Waiting at the top of the sheet gets the number + amount.
  • A CHECK / SAVING row whose permit is no longer waiting becomes DONE by itself.

Make voucherRibbon · Sale Voucher
↑ picture

Makes the sale voucher of every waiting permit, one by one, oldest first - one permit per Save. It reads PACS's list again and asks first in the "please check" window.

What happens

  1. "Please check" window: Godown, Activity, Payment, permits, saves, login date, batch. Cancel has the focus - click the green button (e.g. "Make 3 sale vouchers").
  2. Each saved permit gets a DONE row with its amount, Batch ID and time.
  3. At the end: "Sale vouchers made for 3 of 3 permit(s)."

Good to know

  • The selected cell does not matter - this button works on PACS's waiting list, not on the sheet rows.
  • A refused Save (ERROR) or an unclear one (CHECK) stops the run - the message says why. Fix it and press Make voucher again: the permit is still in PACS's list.

Pause / ResumeRibbon · Run (selected row)
↑ picture

During Make voucher: waits after the current voucher (between two Saves). The button then says Resume - press it to go on. After a pause the app reads PACS's list again.

StopRibbon · Run (selected row)
↑ picture

The current voucher finishes (a Save is never cut off), then the run stops - the message starts with "Stopped by you." The permits not made stay in PACS's list for the next Make voucher.

Clear SheetRibbon · Run (selected row)
↑ picture

Deletes all log rows of this sheet when it gets long. It asks first (No is the default) and keeps a copy of the file in Desktop \ PACS Auto Files \ Backups. The vouchers on PACS are not touched.

Good to know

  • Not possible while a run is going on.
  • Ctrl+Z cannot bring the rows back - use the copy.

Before you clear

  • Press Check pending voucher once - CHECK rows that were saved turn DONE.

5. The columns

All columns are filled by the app - you type nothing here.

ColumnWhat the app writes
ActivitySales or Sale Returns (the choice at the top).
Permit No, Permit DateThe permit (PACS's Bill No.) and its date.
Godown, PaymentThe choices of the run.
AmountPACS's own amount after its round-off - the app never rounds by itself.
Batch IDThe new batch made for this Save (Credit, Cash and Credit). Empty for Cash.
Saved OnDate and time of the Save.
STATUS, MESSAGEThe result - see part 7.

6. Step by step

1Activity, Godown, Payment→2Check pending voucher→3Make voucher→4please check → click→5DONE→6Post on PACS
  1. Choose Activity, Godown and Payment at the top of the sheet.
  2. Check pending voucher - a read-only look at the permits waiting. Nothing is saved.
  3. Make voucher - the app reads the list again and shows the "please check" window.
  4. Read it and click the green button (e.g. "Make 3 sale vouchers").
  5. Watch the rows turn DONE, one per permit. Need a break? Pause / Stop - they act between two Saves.
  6. Post the vouchers on PACS yourself.

8. Good to know

  • One permit per Save, oldest first - the same order a person would follow on PACS.
  • The app works on PACS's waiting list, not on rows you type - so a permit can never get two vouchers from the app.
  • Saved vouchers are final on PACS; posting is yours.

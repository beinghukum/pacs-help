Sale Cancel

Cancels whole permits on PACS. You type only the Permit No; the app first finds each permit on PACS and shows you the member, khad and amount - nothing is cancelled until you check them and click.

1. What this module does

It works on the PACS page Merchandise → Sale Cancel (Sale Type: Sales). For every Permit No it cancels the whole permit - every khad line, the full quantity - with the Reason you chose, then writes DONE, the reason and the time into the row.

A cancelled permit cannot be brought back. Read the "please check" window carefully.
Sale Cancel never changes your Khad Sale sheet. If you keep a note there, change it yourself.

2. Before you start

  • Logged in to PACS - green Logged in (see Getting started).
  • The permit's sale voucher is not made yet. PACS lists only such permits for cancelling.
  • You know the Permit No (PACS calls it Bill No.) and roughly the date of the permit.

Sale Cancel - ribbon
Cancel PermitsRibbon · Sale Cancel
↑ picture

Cancels on PACS every permit of this sheet that is not DONE (Sale type Sales, with the Godown, Reason and dates chosen at the top). The selected row does not matter. It finds the permits on PACS first and asks before cancelling.

What happens

  1. Look-up (read only): Member Name, Permit Amount and Items in Permit are filled, MESSAGE "Found on PACS - not cancelled yet".
  2. "Please check" window with the permits. Go back has the focus and Enter does nothing - click the red button (e.g. "Cancel 2 permits").
  3. One permit per Save. Each one becomes DONE with Reason and Cancelled On.

Skipped

  • Rows already DONE, and CHECK / SAVING rows (look at them on PACS first).
  • A Permit No typed twice - the second row gets ERROR.
  • A permit not found on PACS - ERROR (part 7).
  • The window names the skipped ones in red.

6. Step by step

1Type Permit No→2Godown, Reason, Dates→3Cancel Permits→4App finds them on PACS→5please check → click→6DONE
  1. Type the Permit No of each permit to cancel, one per row.
  2. Choose Godown, Reason and the From / To dates (or Dates).
  3. Press Cancel Permits.
  4. Look-up (read only): the app fills Member Name, Permit Amount and Items in Permit. Nothing is cancelled yet.
  5. The "please check" window lists the permits with member, khad and amount. Check them, then click the red button.
  6. Each cancelled permit becomes DONE. A CHECK stops the run so you can look first.

8. Good to know

  • The whole permit is always cancelled, and Sale Type is always Sales. A returned permit goes through Sale Return.
  • If PACS logs out before a Save, the app logs in again once (captcha) and goes on; after a Save it never presses Save a second time.
  • Before every Save the app checks that PACS is still logged in to your society.
